02 — Historical Development

History of power production

How has the global energy mix developed over time? Scroll through the narrative or select a country to explore.

1990 – The Fossil Era. At the start of our timeline, coal, oil, and gas dominated global power generation grids. Renewable alternatives were tiny fractions of total output.

2002 – Shifting Baseloads. Hydroelectric power held steady as a reliable foundation, while natural gas began replacing more carbon-heavy coal infrastructures globally.

2014 – The Clean Tech Dawn. Thanks to manufacturing scaling up, production costs for wind and solar plummeted. Modern capacity expansions began shifting upwards.

2026 – The Turning Point. Today, clean tech represents the fastest-growing sector on earth. In Austria, the domestic clean mix has surged past 80% towards full sustainability.
